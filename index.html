02 / BEHIND THE PAGES

One operator.
An entire era.

Published in 1923, Edwin Lefèvre’s novel follows the fictional trader Larry Livingston, a character inspired by Jesse Livermore. Its enduring subject is the person behind every market decision.

This independent companion draws on themes in the book. The supplied 2010 Wiley edition includes annotations by Jon D. Markman. These lesson summaries are original, and are not excerpts from that commentary.

01

The making of an observer

From the quotation board to the bucket shops, Livingston learns to notice patterns—and the limits of his experience.

02

A different kind of market

Real exchange trading brings execution delays, scale, and consequences that his early methods did not anticipate.

03

The hardest opponent

Success and failure return him to the same problem: managing his own confidence, impatience, and susceptibility to others.
